# RISKS.md — Perp DEX points-farming risk register

One line each, severity rated for a 1,000 EUR retail delta-neutral farming position.
Not exhaustive, not financial or tax advice. Read alongside `FARMING_COST.md`.

| Risk | Severity | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Sybil disqualification** | HIGH — total loss of farmed points/allocation | Multi-account, VPN, or bot-pattern detection can zero out an entire farming campaign retroactively, with no appeal in most programs; none of the 10 venues researched published a Sybil-detection methodology in their own docs (unsurprising — publishing it would defeat it), so this risk is fundamentally unquantifiable in advance. |
| **Program terms change unilaterally** | HIGH — cost already sunk, payoff redefined after the fact | Extended's own docs explicitly state its points program "may be updated, paused, or brought to an end" at any time with no fixed date (see `venues.csv`) — this is the most transparent venue in the set on this exact risk, and it still reserves the right. Assume every other venue reserves the same right even where undocumented. |
| **Smart-contract exploit** | HIGH — potential total loss of deposited capital | All 10 venues are relatively young protocols on L2s/appchains; several (GRVT, Nado, Extended, StandX) have limited public audit history findable from this research pass. Capital sits at risk of protocol-level exploit for the full farming duration, independent of trading P&L. |
| **Venue insolvency / withdrawal freeze** | HIGH — capital could become inaccessible for an unknown period, or permanently | Perp DEXs intermediate margin and PnL settlement; an insurance-fund shortfall or bridge/sequencer failure can freeze withdrawals. This is a real, historically-observed failure mode across the broader DeFi perp-DEX category, not a hypothetical. |
| **TGE never happens** | MEDIUM-HIGH — full farming cost incurred for zero payoff | Modeled explicitly in the speculative scenario grid (`results/farm_speculative_scenario_grid.csv`) via the TGE-probability axis (100/50/25%). Several researched venues (Pacifica, Nado, Reya, StandX, GRVT's Season 2, Paradex Season 3) have never confirmed a token at all — this is not a tail risk for those, it is an open question. |
| **Token dumps at unlock / post-TGE price decline** | MEDIUM-HIGH — realized value can be far below the FDV/price used to size the allocation | Modeled via the haircut axis (0/-50/-80%) in the scenario grid. This is an extremely common, widely-observed pattern for airdropped tokens generally (broad-based farmed allocations create large, price-insensitive sell pressure at unlock) — the -50% and -80% cases should not be treated as extreme tail scenarios. |
| **Stranded capital across venues during rebalance** | MEDIUM | A rebalancing transfer that is delayed (bridge congestion, sequencer downtime) leaves one leg under-margined while the transfer is in flight, during which that leg's liquidation risk is temporarily higher than the steady-state numbers in `results/farm_liquidation_probabilities.csv`. Not separately quantified here. |
| **Liquidation of one leg (delta-neutral is not liquidation-proof)** | MEDIUM-HIGH, leverage-dependent | Directly quantified in `results/farm_liquidation_probabilities.csv`: at 3x leverage, 32% (BTC) to 65% (ETH) probability of at least one leg being liquidated within a month, using this project's own realized-volatility data. A liquidated leg strands the OTHER leg's gain on a different venue while crystallizing a real loss on the liquidated one — this is not a wash even though the position was "delta-neutral" by construction. |
| **Fee/points-formula opacity** | MEDIUM | Several venues (Paradex Season 3, StandX, Nado, GRVT) have not published exact points-per-dollar-volume formulas from primary sources found in this research — the $-cost side of this report is precise, but the points/eventual-allocation side is not just probabilistically unknown (Task 5), it is *mechanically* unknown for how points convert to tokens at several venues. |
| **German tax treatment of airdrops** | MEDIUM, compliance not financial risk | Airdrops are generally treated as income at the time of receipt under German tax law (not solely as a capital gain realized on eventual sale), and trading activity itself may have separate reporting implications. **This is not tax advice — confirm actual treatment with a qualified German tax advisor before farming, receiving, or selling any allocation.** Nothing in this report should be used to estimate a post-tax outcome. |

## What this register deliberately does not do
It does not assign a combined probability of "something going wrong" — the risks are not
independent, several are correlated with market conditions (a broad crypto downturn raises TGE
delay risk, dump risk, and liquidation risk simultaneously), and false precision here would
undermine the one thing this run was asked to do reliably: price the cost side honestly.

# Data acquisition priorities

| Priority | Family | Examples | New information | Minimum history | Point-in-time requirements | Current status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Institutional flows | Spot BTC/ETH ETF flows; CME positioning; CFTC positioning; CoinShares flow reports; exchange reserve changes | Regulated/institutional demand and inventory pressure not captured by Binance OHLCV or macro gates. | Daily or weekly 2019-2026 preferred; ETF flows start 2024, so require explicit limited-sample treatment. | Publication timestamp, fund identifier mapping, AUM denominator vintage, holiday handling, and no revised backfill without vintage labels. | Not acquired in usable structured form; prior module found no usable ETF-flow time series. |

| 3 | Options-implied information | Implied volatility; skew; risk reversals; put/call ratios; volatility term structure | Forward-looking risk premia, crash insurance demand, upside call demand, and term-structure stress. | Daily BTC/ETH constant-maturity surfaces through development and holdout. | Quote timestamp, stale quote filters, expiry/strike survivorship, settlement convention, and surface construction reproducibility. | Public Deribit historical-vol endpoint was reachable but too short for 2019-2026; no options feature accepted. |
| 4 | Cross-sectional market structure | Sector leadership; breadth expansion; correlation regimes; dominance measures; relative-strength leadership | Rotation, breadth diffusion, and leadership state across the crypto universe. | Point-in-time universe with liquidity, sector taxonomy, and delisting/survivorship controls. | Lagged universe construction, sector taxonomy vintage, stable/wrapped exclusion, and reconstitution audit. | Existing crypto-native leadership features were researched; prior expansion overlay did not improve the frozen strategy economically. |

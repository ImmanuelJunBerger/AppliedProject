# Crypto ML strategy-research overview

## Scope and evidence standard

This document audits the implemented research in the repository. Synthetic demonstration results are excluded from economic conclusions. The real-data panel is Binance spot USDT OHLCV from 2019-01-01 through 2026-06-22, with 415 downloaded assets, 524,164 observations, 53.73% full-panel missingness and 0.09% missingness within each asset's listing span. Point-in-time universes reduce look-ahead, but the current exchange catalogue cannot recover every delisted pair; survivorship risk remains.

The 2025-01-01 onward period was initially locked, but it has now been opened in several completed studies. It remains valid evidence about those frozen experiments. It is **not an untouched holdout for any new hypothesis designed after this audit**.

## Cross-project conclusions

1. **The strongest replicated forecast is volatility expansion, not return direction.** Its price-only signal survived the locked holdout with good discrimination and calibration.
2. **Prediction and economic value are separate gates.** The volatility forecast did not reliably improve portfolio outcomes after costs and exposure effects.
3. **Base-signal quality is the binding constraint.** Allocation and meta-labeling degraded a weak signal mix; flexible ML filters did not rescue breakout or momentum.
4. **Cash and volatility controls reduced drawdown but also removed return.** Exposure must be reported alongside Sharpe and drawdown.
5. **The original holdout is consumed.** New strategy specifications require a prospective holdout after 2026-06-22.
6. **Feature work should focus on conditional opportunity.** Dispersion, residual momentum, trend consistency, crowding and correlation breakdown have clearer mechanisms than another broad model search.

## Scientific debt to resolve

- Preserve delisted-market history and archived exchange metadata to reduce survivorship bias.
- Define event time, publication time and live availability for every non-price field.
- Separate feature discovery from strategy evaluation; every tested threshold and variant must enter the configuration count.
- Replace same-return subtraction with rolling factor residualization for residual momentum.
- Add provider and venue sensitivity before trusting derivatives, stablecoin or microstructure features.
- Begin a prospective, immutable prediction and portfolio log before opening a new holdout.
